Music for Telugu cinema. Over 250 short films.

PVR Raja is a composer, record producer, songwriter and guitarist working in Telugu cinema.

Known as the “Short Films Maestro” and the “Ilaiyaraaja of Short Films”, he began by composing music for over 250 short films and independent films.

He holds an India Book of Records entry for composing music for the most Telugu short films by an individual.
